04 Questions

Good to know.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks. A multi-page site or web app can take 3–6 weeks, depending on content and features.

Can you add features to a bot I already have?

Yes. I can extend an existing Discord.js bot, add new commands, or move its data to a better database. I'll review the code first and tell you what's needed.

Do you provide hosting?

I can set up hosting for you, or deploy to a platform you already use. Ongoing hosting costs depend on the platform and your traffic.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a short support window for bug fixes. Longer maintenance plans are available too.

FAQ Pricing & process

Every package on the Store page lists what is included and what is extra, so there are no surprises.

How long does a project take?

Discord bots are usually set up in 3 to 7 days. Websites usually take 1 to 3 weeks, depending on the number of pages and how much content is ready.

How do I pay?

Packages marked "Order online" can be paid by UPI. Log in, place the order, then open My orders. Pay the amount by UPI, upload the screenshot and enter the UTR number. Your payment is confirmed after it is checked.

What is not included in a package?

Each package shows a "Not included" note, for example domain name, hosting or monthly fees. Anything not listed in the package is quoted separately before work starts.

Are revisions included?

The number of revisions is agreed in writing before work starts, so you know what to expect.

Can I order something custom?

Yes. Send a message from the Contact page with your idea, timeline and budget. You will get a quote and a written agreement before any payment.

How is my information used?

Your details are used only to reply to your enquiry and to manage your order. See the privacy policy for more.
