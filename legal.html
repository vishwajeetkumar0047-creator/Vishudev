You add packages to your cart and place the order at checkout. Free packages cannot be refunded because nothing was paid.

For packages you can claim online, the product appears on the product page once the order is marked as completed. Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Information you give us

When you create an account we store your email address. Your password is stored by our login provider as a secure hash, and we never see it. When you contact us we store your name, email, project details and message. When you order, we store the order details, the payment amount, the UTR number and the payment screenshot you upload.

2. Where your data is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase database and file storage in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region. Payment screenshots and product files are stored in private storage. Only you and our admin can open them.

3. How we use information

We use your information to reply to your enquiries, manage your orders and payments, give you your product, send order-related emails, and protect the site from spam and abuse.

4. Cookies, storage and tracking

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site keeps your login session in your browser so you stay logged in. It also stores your cart and your light or dark theme choice in your browser. We count page views to understand which pages are useful. These counts are not linked to you, and we do not store your IP address in our database.

5. Third-party services

We use these services to run the site: Supabase (database, login and file storage), Resend (sending emails), jsDelivr (loads our JavaScript libraries), and Google Fonts (loads the typefaces). Payments are made in your own UPI app, so your bank details are never sent to us.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers listed above, or when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep data

We keep account information while your account is active. Order and payment records are kept for accounting and tax purposes. Contact messages are kept for up to 2 years, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

8. Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and messages. Email us at vishwajeetkumar0047@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

The site is not meant for children under 18. If you are under 18, please use the site only with a parent or guardian.
